# Market Context: Denmark (DK)

> Load this file when answering any Denmark-specific regulatory or operational question.

## Regulatory Framework

| Law / Rule | Scope |
|------------|-------|
| Færdselsloven | Road traffic and parking law |
| Parkeringsbekendtgørelsen | Parking regulations (public roads) |
| Private parking regulation | Self-regulated via BrancheOrganisationen for Parkeringsbranchen |
| Inkassoloven (DK) | Debt collection regulations |
| GDPR (via Databeskyttelsesloven) | Data privacy |

## Key Operational Facts

- **Regulator:** No single national regulator for private parking; industry self-regulation via BrancheOrg
- **Enforcement type:** Private control fee (`afgift` or `parkeringsafgift`) — contractual
- **Currency:** DKK
- **Max control fee:** Typically 750–1,000 DKK (industry standard, not legally capped for private)
- **Grace period:** Not legally mandated; industry best practice is 10 minutes
- **Appeal deadline:** Typically 15 days; not uniformly regulated
- **Evidence requirement:** Similar to Norway — photo evidence strongly recommended to defend disputes

## Differences vs. Norway

| Aspect | Norway | Denmark |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Regulator | SVV (government) | BrancheOrg (industry) |
| Fee cap | Legally set (1,000 NOK) | Industry guideline only |
| Grace period | Legally required | Best practice only |
| Vipps equivalent | Vipps | MobilePay (dominant) |
| ANPR maturity | High | Moderate, growing |

## Payment & Billing

- **MobilePay** is the dominant mobile payment method — expected by operators
- Card and bank transfer common
- Parking discs (parkeringsskive) still used in some zones — relevant for time-limit enforcement without ANPR

## Data & Privacy

- Plate data = personal data under GDPR (Denmark)
- Datatilsynet is the Danish DPA
- ANPR footage retention: typically 30 days

## ANPR Specifics

- Danish plates: format `AB 12 345` (2 letters + 2 digits + 3 digits)
- Electric vehicles: green plate strip — `EL` not embedded in plate text
- Confidence threshold: same industry standard ~95%

## Regional Notes

- Copenhagen (København): largest market; complex zone structures; heavy cycling culture affects parking demand
- Aarhus, Odense, Aalborg: significant secondary markets
- Seasonal variation less extreme than Norway (no heavy snow belt)
