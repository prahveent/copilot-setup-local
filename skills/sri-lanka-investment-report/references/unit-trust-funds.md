## Key Facts for Reports

- Minimum investment across all open-end unit trusts: **Rs. 1,000**
- Unit trusts are **NOT** covered by SLDIS deposit insurance (unlike bank FDs)
- Applications received before **3:00 PM** on a business day get that day's NAV
- Redemption proceeds typically paid within **3–10 business days**
- Minimum 100 units typically must remain after partial redemption
- All AMCs must be licensed by the **Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) of Sri Lanka**
- Trustee bank is **independent** from the fund manager — a key safety structure
- CAL IGF is the **only rated unit trust fund** in Sri Lanka (Fitch + ICRA rated)
- First Capital Equity Fund has the **best 5-year track record** (CAGR 34.23%)
- CALI is the **largest AMC** in Sri Lanka by AUM (Rs. 180Bn+ as of recent data)
